# Research: Bolsonaro's Impact on LGBTQ+ Rights in Brazil

## Overview

This repository contains comprehensive research documentation examining the impact of former Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro's rhetoric, policies, and actions on LGBTQ+ individuals in Brazil.

## Key Research Questions Addressed

- How did Bolsonaro's pre-election comments resonate with voters?
- What measurable impacts occurred during his presidency?
- What data exists on violence and discrimination against LGBTQ+ individuals?
- How long might his rhetoric's effects persist after his departure?
- What are the possibilities of his political return?
- How have his actions empowered certain attitudes within families and communities?

## Important Notes

### Limitations
- Official government statistics on LGBTQ+ violence are limited
- Under-reporting is a significant factor
- COVID-19 pandemic affected both actual patterns and reporting mechanisms
- Causal attribution is methodologically complex

### Context
- This is a compilation of publicly available information and research
- The political situation continues to evolve
- Readers should consult primary sources for the most current information
- The document aims for objectivity while addressing sensitive topics
