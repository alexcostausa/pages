About This Research

This research compilation addresses critical questions about the intersection of political rhetoric and human rights in Brazil:

  • How did anti-LGBTQ+ rhetoric contribute to electoral success?
  • What measurable changes occurred in violence and discrimination?
  • How long will these effects persist after political transition?
  • What role do families play in perpetuating or challenging these attitudes?
  • What are the scenarios for political return and continued influence?

Data Sources: Information compiled from Brazilian civil society organizations (Grupo Gay da Bahia, ANTRA), international human rights groups (Human Rights Watch, Amnesty International), academic research, documented public statements, and legal proceedings.

Research Methodology

This document synthesizes information from multiple credible sources to provide a comprehensive overview of:

  • Historical Context: Pre-election statements and their political impact
  • During-Presidency Analysis: Policy changes, statistics, and social climate
  • Long-Term Projections: Academic estimates of lasting effects
  • Current Situation: Legal status and political landscape
  • Future Scenarios: Possibilities for political return or continued influence
Important Note: This research addresses sensitive topics with objectivity and respect. It acknowledges limitations in available data and the ongoing nature of political developments. The document is intended for educational and informational purposes.
